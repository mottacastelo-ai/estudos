straight edges. Two thin horizontal silver lines (1px each) separate it from the
card body above and below. The panel surface has an extremely subtle cross-hatch
texture. This area is for text — leave it clean.

CORNER ORNAMENTS (all 4 corners): Small silver diamond shapes (12px, 4-pointed)
with a single pixel dot at center. Clean, minimal, elegant.

ACCENT DETAILS: Two very thin vertical lines in pale lavender (#A78BFA, 20% opacity)
running the full card height on left and right inner edges (just inside the frame).

COLOR PALETTE: #1A1035 (base), #1E1B4B (panel), silver/pewter, #A78BFA (accent
hairlines), white (circle glow). No warm tones. Cool and composed.

No text, no characters, no logos. Pure frame and atmosphere.
