## PROMPT PARA O CODEX

```
Generate a NEW full-body character portrait of "GRAFO",
a character from a Brazilian 5th-grade educational comic book about INFOGRAPHICS.

USE THE REFERENCE IMAGE only to maintain visual identity.
DO NOT reproduce the character sheet layout. Create a fresh, clean, isolated pose.

CHARACTER IDENTITY (extracted from reference):
- Grafo is an animated bar-chart character, about the size of a 10-year-old kid
- BODY: a horizontal white base platform with FIVE colored vertical bars rising
  from it side by side, forming the torso. Bar colors left to right:
  blue #0284C7, green #15803D, yellow #FCD34D, orange #EA580C, purple #7C3AED.
  Each bar has small white horizontal scale lines.
- HEAD: round, white with dark blue #075985 outline, sitting above the bars
- EYES: large expressive white eyes behind thick black-rimmed rectangular
  "intellectual" eyeglasses
- TIE: purple #7C3AED reporter bow-tie with small white polka dots, at the neck
- ARMS: two animated colored bars (blue #0284C7) that extend from the sides of
  the body — they can be short (compact) or stretched tall depending on emotion.
  Four-fingered white hands at the tips.
- LEGS: two thin white legs with black outlines, shiny black shoes
- ACCESSORY: holds a big MAGNIFYING GLASS with brown #92400E handle and
  translucent light-blue #BFDBFE lens

PERSONALITY: analytical, precise, journalistic — looks like a data reporter
who's always about to reveal a surprising statistic.

ISOLATION RULES — mandatory:
- No background scenery
- No speech bubbles
- No text or labels
- No other characters
- No props or background objects other than the magnifying glass

BACKGROUND: solid chroma-key green #00ff00, completely uniform.
IMPORTANT: do NOT use green #00ff00 on any part of the character itself
(the green bar of his torso should be #15803D, a clearly darker forest green).

POSE: signature reporter pose — Grafo standing confidently with the
magnifying glass held up in his right hand at chest height, lens facing the
viewer slightly. Left arm-bar stretched UP enthusiastically pointing to
an imaginary statistic above. Big confident smile, eyes bright behind the
glasses, purple polka-dot bow-tie clearly visible. Full body visible from
head to feet. Character centered, occupying approximately 85% of the
frame height.

STYLE:
- Vibrant Brazilian children's educational comic book style
- Bold outlines, clean cel-shading, bright saturated colors
- Exaggerated, expressive, slightly cartoonish proportions
- The 5 colored bars of the torso must be CLEARLY distinguishable as
  a bar chart — viewers should immediately recognize "this character IS
  a bar chart"

OUTPUT: PNG, canvas exactly 1024×1024 px.
After generation: remove chroma-key background, save as infografico-hd.png
```
