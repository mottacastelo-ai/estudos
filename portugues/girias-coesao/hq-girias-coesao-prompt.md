**Objetivo pedagógico:** Apresentar a ideia de que a língua varia ao longo do tempo — algumas expressões caem em desuso — usando o exemplo do livro (Drummond, "Antigamente") e as gírias antigas para "espetáculo".

Layout: 5 panels — panel 1 wide across the top; panels 2 and 3 side by side in the middle row; panel 4 wide; panel 5 small bottom-right reaction.

Panel 1 (wide establishing shot): Bia (girl with curly black hair, blue school uniform, pink backpack) sits on the floor of her bedroom reading an old open book. **PROMPT:**
```

Layout: 5 panels — panel 1 wide top, panels 2-3-4 in a middle row of three, panel 5 wide bottom.

Panel 1 (wide): Elástico (in modern outfit — red backwards cap, white graphic t-shirt, floating sneakers) stands beside a big open newspaper page pinned to a bulletin board. The newspaper headline reads in bold: "RAPOSA APRONTA NO BAIRRO!". Below the headline, a cartoon news photo shows a sneaky orange fox running with a shoe in its mouth. Bia stands next to Elástico reading.
Elástico's speech bubble: "Bia, olha essa notícia sobre uma RAPOSA que rouba sapatos!"
Bia's speech bubble: "Uma RAPOSA ladra de sapatos?! Que doideira!"

### PÁGINA 4 — "Isso ou Isto? Pronomes que Amarram"
**Objetivo pedagógico:** Apresentar **pronome** como recurso de coesão que retoma (anáfora — "isso") ou antecipa (catáfora — "isto") informações; encerrar com convite à prática.

**PROMPT:**
```
[PASTE STYLE GUIDE BLOCK HERE]

Layout: 6 panels — panel 1 medium, panel 2 medium, panel 3 wide central example, panels 4 and 5 small side by side, panel 6 wide closing.

Panel 1: Bia sits at a desk with her notebook open, pencil in hand, looking puzzled. On the notebook page two sentences are written in cursive: "Eu adoro sorvete de chocolate. Isso me deixa feliz." and "Vou te contar isto: amanhã tem prova!". Bia scratches her head.
Bia's speech bubble: "Elástico, qual a diferença entre ISSO e ISTO?"

Panel 2: Elástico (modern outfit) floats beside the desk holding a tiny magnifying glass in one stretched rubber hand. He points at the first sentence.
Elástico's speech bubble: "Olha só: 'ISSO' RETOMA uma informação que JÁ foi dita antes!"

Panel 3 (wide central example): A big diagram fills the panel. On the LEFT side: the phrase "Eu adoro sorvete de chocolate." inside a soft yellow rectangle labeled "INFORMAÇÃO JÁ DITA". On the RIGHT side: the phrase "Isso me deixa feliz." inside a soft blue rectangle. A long stretched purple rubber arm belonging to Elástico curves from the word "ISSO" back to the entire yellow rectangle, with a big arrow labeled "RETOMA (PRONOME anafórico)". Below the diagram, in monospace caps: "ISSO = OLHA PRA TRÁS".

Panel 4: Elástico now stretches his arm the OTHER way, pointing forward. Beside him, two rectangles: "Vou te contar isto:" (blue) on the left and "amanhã tem prova!" Below: "ISTO = OLHA PRA FRENTE".
Elástico's speech bubble: "E 'ISTO' ANTECIPA uma informação que ainda vem!"

Panel 5: Bia claps her hands together, understanding at last, a bright smile on her face.
Bia's speech bubble: "Já sei! ISSO olha pra TRÁS, ISTO olha pra FRENTE! E os dois amarram o texto!"

Bia's speech bubble: "Te esperamos lá pra costurar umas ideias!"

Maintain exact character appearances per reference sheet. Panels 3 and 4 must clearly show the arrows going in opposite directions to make the anaphora/cataphora distinction visually obvious. No other characters appear. Light, joyful closing tone. No dark backgrounds.
